Bhavik Kumar

PhD Student in Quantum Computation and Information at University of Copenhagen, exploring quantum simulation and many-body dynamics.

About Me

I am passionate about advancing the field of quantum computation and information with a focus on quantum simulation of many-body physics. My research combines theoretical and numerical methods to devise novel protocols for analyzing and mitigating noise in quantum systems using tools from quantum learning theory.

To learn more, connect with me on LinkedIn or view my Google Scholar profile.
